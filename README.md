## What HBX Is

An open-core stack for national digital infrastructure:

- 🆔 Identity & authentication (HBX-Identity)
- 🏛️ Public-service portals (HBX-Portal)
- 📚 Education systems (HBX-Edu)
- 🏥 Health records (HBX-Health)
- 🏢 Business registration (HBX-BizReg)
- 🗺️ Land records (HBX-Land)
- 💰 Tax systems (HBX-Tax)
- 📊 Project dashboards (HBX-Projects)
- 📋 Open-data systems (HBX-Data)
- 🚨 Emergency communication (HBX-Alert)

Built and maintained by **HBX-Forge** — an autonomous build engine that plans, codes, tests, secures, and deploys the whole thing with human approval gates at life-safety boundaries.
